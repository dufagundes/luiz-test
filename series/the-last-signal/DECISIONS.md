## How to add a result
1. Post the chapter with its poll (see `chapters/chXX/POST.md`).
2. When voting closes, tell Claude the real result, e.g. *"Chapter 2 winner: 1 (Open the door), 62% of 1,240 votes"*. From Chapter 2 on, choices are numbered **1 / 2** (Chapter 1 used A / B).
3. Claude records it here, updates `SEASON_1_OUTLINE.md` and the Story Bible, and produces the next chapter.
