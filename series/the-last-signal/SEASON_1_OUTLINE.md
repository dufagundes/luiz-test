# THE LAST SIGNAL: Season 1 Outline (12 chapters)

**Series description (for profiles and playlists):**
> At 11:47 PM, every phone in Harlow Point gets the same message: *"You have 24 hours. Do not trust the person closest to you."* It came from the future, from a version of this town where almost everyone is gone. **You vote on what happens next.** New chapter after every poll.

**Season arcs**
- **A: The Signal mystery.** Who sent it, what the tower does, what happens at the deadline.
- **B: Nora and Theo.** Trust broken in Ch.1, tested by his Echo, rebuilt (or not) by the finale.
- **C: The Vance family.** Milo's dreams and the truth about Elias.
- **D: Nora and Jules.** Former friends forced to work together. The sheriff's cover-up.

Each chapter is roughly 2 hours of story time. Countdown values are approximate targets.

> ⚠️ **Flexible by design.** Chapters 2–12 are *planned*, not locked. Each audience vote changes the route. The fixed points (★) happen no matter what. The *way* we reach them depends on the votes. Branch notes show how each option plays out.

### Ch.1: "11:47" ✅ produced · countdown 24:00:00 → 23:59:15
Every phone lights up. Dad's radio turns on with a voice that sounds like Nora. Theo climbs in. Milo shows a drawing he made *yesterday*, with a dark figure behind Theo. The radio says: *"Don't trust Theo. He got the message three weeks ago. Come to the tower. Alone."* Theo's phone proves it. The town blacks out and the tower turns white.
**Reveal:** the message came early to Theo, and the voice is Nora's. **Cliffhanger:** blackout plus a betrayal.
**POLL:** *Theo lied for 3 weeks. What should Nora do?* **A: Let Theo explain** · **B: Go to the tower alone**

Nora is hurt ("And you just… didn't tell me?") but believes him. Milo's new drawing: the boy with Theo's glasses, a phone reading 11:47, "he dosnt get wet". Only the sheriff's station still has power. A text from Theo's number: *"I'm outside. Don't trust whoever is with you."* In the yard: a second Theo, bone-dry in the pouring rain, phone frozen at 11:47.
**Reveal:** the second message, plus the Echo tells (never wet, 11:47). **Twist / cliffhanger:** two Theos.
**POLL:** *The other Theo is outside. What should Nora do?* **1: Open the door** · **2: Run to the station** (new interactive ending: comment 1 or 2, follow for Ch.3)

### Ch.3: "Don't Let Him In" ✅ produced (route 1, creator's choice) · countdown 23:57:43 → 23:56:57
**Ch.2 ending choice 1, "Open the door", selected by the creator (not an audience vote).** Hook: lock click, door swings open, lightning: the dry Theo on the porch: "Hi, Nora." He can't cross the threshold (barrier). He makes the real Theo confess he was at the tower 3 years ago and saw Elias walk into the light and the Echo walk out (desaturated flashback). The Echo slides a photo across the threshold: the tower, Elias, young Theo, and 13-year-old Nora in her yellow raincoat. "That's me… but I was never there." The radio upstairs: "Nora. Close the door. Now." The Echo stops smiling.
**Major revelation:** Theo witnessed Elias vanish, and the Echo has existed for 3 years. **Twist:** Nora was there too. **Cliffhanger:** radio vs. Echo.
**POLL:** *The radio says shut it. He has answers. What should Nora do?* **1: Close the door** · **2: Let him in**

### Ch.4: "No Reflection" · ~23:50 left (planned, adapts to the Ch.3 vote)
- **If 1 (close the door):** Nora slams it. The Echo's handprint stays on the glass from outside. The radio voice praises her, then asks her to bring the photo to the tower. Theo notices the hall mirror never showed the Echo (rule: no reflection) and tests everyone with his phone camera. Jules calls: "My mom just left the station. Toward the tower." Cost: the Echo's answers are lost and he's now hostile, following them.
- **If 2 (let him in):** "Come in." The barrier drops, the lights flicker, and the Echo steps inside, dripping nothing. He tells Nora she has an Echo too ("You weren't the only Nora up there") and that the radio voice is lying. Cost: once invited, an Echo can enter any time, and the real Theo refuses to stay in the same room. The mirror shows only three people in a hall of four.
Either way Ch.4 confirms the **no-reflection** rule and brings **Jules** in (phone call or arrival).

### Ch.5: "Cutter's Hill" · ~17:00 left
★ The group reaches the tower. Beneath it is the **Meridian bunker**, with Dad's handwriting on the wall: *"DON'T LET HER BROADCAST."*
**Cliffhanger:** the bunker door seals, with one of them on the wrong side.
**Poll idea:** depends on who is trapped (vote-driven).

### Ch.6: "Two Theos" ★ MIDSEASON · ~14:00 left
★ Two identical Theos. Both wet, both know their shared memories. The audience votes on which one is real, using clues hidden in Ch.1–5.
**Consequence:** a wrong vote means the Echo travels with the group into Ch.7–9 (a hidden traitor storyline). A right vote means the Echo escapes and hunts them.
**Poll:** *Which Theo is real? A: the one holding the radio · B: the one bleeding*

### Ch.7: "Dad's Notebook" · ~11:30 left
★ Elias's logbook: the tower is a two-way door. At 11:47 PM it folds the town into the future static. He went through to stop it from the other side.
**Cliffhanger:** the last page is in *Nora's* handwriting, dated next year.

### Ch.8: "The Quiet Street" · ~9:00 left
★ Maple Street starts disappearing house by house: people, cars, sound. Mom (Ruth Vance) vanishes mid-sentence on the phone.
**Cliffhanger:** the countdown jumps forward 2 hours.

### Ch.9: "Milo Draws the End" · ~6:30 left
★ Milo draws his final picture: Nora stepping into the white light at the top of the tower, with someone smiling behind her. He's terrified to show it.
**Cliffhanger:** the person smiling in the drawing is Nora.

### Ch.10: "The Sheriff's Secret" · ~4:00 left
★ Dana Okafor confesses the night Elias vanished. She saw *two* Noras at the tower that night (Nora was 13). One came home. Jules has to choose between her mother and her friends.
**Poll idea:** *Trust the sheriff / Lock her in the station*

### Ch.11: "Transmission" · ~1:30 left
★ Nora finally talks to the radio voice face-to-face, in a window of white static at the top of the tower. Future Nora looks exactly like her and is never wet.
**Reveal:** she's the Echo. She needs Nora to broadcast the Last Signal so they swap places.
**Cliffhanger:** behind Future Nora, in the empty future town: **Dad**.

### Ch.12: "The Person Closest to You" ★ FINALE · 0:00:00
★ The deadline. Nora must use the tower herself. The final choice decides the season ending:
- **Ending A, "Close the Door":** Nora broadcasts the true last signal, the Fold collapses, the town is saved, and her Echo fades… but so does Dad's voice.
- **Ending B, "Open the Door":** Nora goes through to bring Dad back, trusting Theo to pull her out. The town survives, but the Nora who comes home is dry in the rain.
**Season 2 hook (either ending):** at 11:47 PM the next night, every phone in a *different* town lights up.

## Fixed-point rules for adapting to votes
1. The four mysteries in the Story Bible (§4) never change. Only *when* and *how* they're uncovered.
2. A choice must have a real consequence in the next chapter (an injury, a lost ally, a clue gained or missed, time lost on the countdown).
3. If a route skips a planned reveal, move it to the next chapter. Never delete it.
4. Record every vote in `DECISIONS.md` before writing the next chapter.
